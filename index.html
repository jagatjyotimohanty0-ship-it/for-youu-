A LITTLE LOVE STORY

Hey Snoor ❤️

Kuch log life mein bas aa jaate hain... aur phir dheere-dheere wahi person tumhari favourite habit ban jaata hai.

01 — THE GIRL
🌹

Tumhari smile ka kya karun?

Tum jab hasta ho na, tab genuinely lagta hai ki duniya thodi aur achhi hai.

Aur haan... tumhare cute nakhre alag hi level ke hain.

02 — OUR LITTLE FIGHTS

Thoda gussa... thode nakhre...

Kabhi tum naraz, kabhi main manaane wala.

Kabhi meri galti, kabhi tumhari... aur kabhi dono ki. 😌

But end mein ek hi rule — baat band nahi karni.

03 — OUR CHAT

Ek normal conversation...

Tum mujhe itna tease kyun karte ho?
Kyunki tumhara reaction dekhna funny hai. 😂
Achhaaa?
Haan... but secretly cute bhi. ❤️
04 — IF I COULD
🌹

Agar tum mere saamne hoti...

Toh shayad main pehle kuch bol hi nahi pata.

Thoda shy hoon na... 😶

Bas ek rose deta, smile karta, aur bolta —

"Mere saath aise hi rehna."
05 — STAY CLOSE

Door ho sakti ho...

Lekin mere liye distance ka matlab kabhi ye nahi raha ki tum door ho.

Calls, texts, random conversations, choti-choti fights, aur woh stupid si hasi...

Ye sab milke hi toh hum bante hain.

06 — MY FAVORITE THING

Tumhe happy dekhna.

Main perfect nahi hoon.

Kabhi stupid mistakes karunga, kabhi tumhe irritate karunga, kabhi tum mujhse gussa hogi.

But ek cheez genuinely chahta hoon —

tumhari smile roz dekhna.
